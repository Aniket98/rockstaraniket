# Iphone Theft Protection - Security Practices

**_A thief does not come by alerting you - so you must be prepared already._**

The below situations can happen:

1. Iphone get stolen and thief turns it off. What you will do?
2. Or worst your iphone is stolen in UNLOCKED state !!
3. Do you know how to protect your data quickly? What if he turns off internet/or set airplane mode in locked state of iphone?
4. How will you track? Do you remember your Apple ID and Password?
5. What if thief starts reading your personal data while iphone is unlocked?
6. What if thief starts changing some iphone settings?

HOW THE HELL will you protect your IPHONE and its "Private DATA"?

Iphone is a very secure device.
But if you are not prepared, you will still lose either your iphone or your data! Period.

We can somehow compensate losing iphone in locked state.
But if iphone is unlocked, then the problem rises. We cannot afford to lose data.

## ----- SECURITY PLAN - IPHONE -----

Before starting, understand the point that to safeguard our data, our first main objective is to "LOCK" the iphone.
Once the iphone is locked, atleast your data is safe. Then, next objective will be to track and get back our iphone.

This is not as simple as it sounds.
If the thief has secretly seen the iphone password you entered earlier, he can Unlock your Iphone.
Do Not Assume that thief has not seen your password secretly. Today's thief are smart. You also don't know how many people are watching your reels scrolling from behind, how will you make sure someone has not seen your password?

Or worst case, if you are standing near Metro Train door, and thief snatches and runs away while iphone was Unlocked.
So, you must be prepared in all cases.

The main flow should be : 

LOCK Iphone -> Trigger lock mode via icloud.com/find -> ERASE Iphone via same website.

The last part to ERASE is important according to me (although its optional to you).
If the iphone gets switched off or become inaccessible to internet, then you cannot lock iphone via icloud.com/find.

Also, in lock mode set via icloud.com/find, once the iphone is unlocked by someone (if thief knows your password), the lock mode will release. And your iphone will become vulnerable again and you need to set the lock mode again. That's why i said ERASING iphone remotely should be crucial.

So, you need many other ways (that i have written below) to lock out the Iphone.

Also, in end, implemented a SMS Trigger to lock the iphone remotely and play a high volume sound that will speak that Iphone is stolen. 

1. Lost iphone: Track on [https://icloud.com/find](https://icloud.com/find) and put iphone in lock mode with a message display to contact you. This will instantly lock your iphone and a message will print on the lock screen that you set via website shown above.
|_ Make sure you have apple ID and password saved already with you safely.
Otherwise you will be unable to do anything.
|_ This works only if your iphone is able to connect to internet. Hence, it is very important to not let thief turn off your internet, which we will see how to do that in next sections.

2. What if thief turns off iphone? Go to [https://icloud.com/find](https://icloud.com/find) and enable lock mode. Then as soon as phone is turned on and connected to internet, it will go in lock mode. The iphone can still be tracked on website even if switched off (This is possible as iphone takes help to track via nearby apple devices in public).

3. But what if thief never enables internet? He may attempt so by putting phone in flight mode or turning off mobile data or wifi from lock screen. To avoid such thing happening, make sure to set your iphone setting already as below:
|_ Dont allow to open Control Center from top during Lock Screen.

4. What if your iphone was in UNLOCKED State when the iphone was stolen?
In this case, the very first thing thief will do is turning off mobile data or wifi or putting in flight mode. To avoid this thing to happen, you must already set in-built iphone automation (using Shortcuts app) to turn ON the mobile data or remove flight mode, the very moment someone changes tries to disable them and just after that iphone should get automatically locked. The automations are given below.
|_ Before you start,,,
You might have already enabled face lock on important apps.
But maybe you have not enabled it to feel more smoothness/comfort in daily life. It's your personal choice where you trade off comfort with added security layer. I leave that decision to you.
But other than this, as discussed, there are few things a thief might do in unlocked state, for which we need to setup below automations to add safety:-
|____ Build and Enable these automations via in-built Shortcuts app:
      i) Lock the iphone automatically when Settings app is opened.
      But what if you wish to open Settings app yourself? Then the iphone will get locked for you as well. Yes, and the answer is simply unlock screen and when you unlock screen you will be inside Settings app and you can do your changes. But for strangers they will not be able to unlock screen and will get stuck.
     ii) Since settings app will be blocked instantly, stranger cannot turn off mobile data or enable airplane mode. But someone can still do so from the Control Center from top on lock screen. (Or if you iphone was stolen in unlocked state).
     To block this, do following:
          |_ a) Turn off Control Center on lock screen. (This can be changed in settings app).
          But if your iphone was stolen in unlocked state, you must setup below also:
          |_ b) BLOCKING Cellular/Mobile Data: --> In ios 27, go to screen time settings, scroll to bottom > Content & Privacy Restrictions > scoll to bottom > Allow changes to Settings > Set cellular data to 'Dont Allow'. Now, from the control center the cellular data cannot be turned off. If you yourself need to switch cellular data on/off, you can do so from settings app since you can pass through via screen lock.
          |_ c) BLOCKING Airplane Mode: 
     iii) Very Important : Make sure the Shortcuts cannot be opened by someone. Now, you cannot enable normal face id to this system app. So, you must do following, in Settings App, go to Screen Time settings, add a screen time passcode. Then, from the screen time app list, block the Shortcuts app. Now, the shortcut apps cannot be opened and will require a passcode to unlock. (Automation Flow : Triggered when airplane mode is turned off --> Execute : Set airplane mode off > Set cellular data on > Lock screen).
     
     |_ Critical Note: If you ever try to open this blocked Shortcuts app, you will need to enter the passcode and open the app. But now the app will become unblocked forever. Either you must block it manually again, or a better way is to set a Time Allow of 0-1 minute to this shortcuts app in the Screen Time settings, this way when you open app forcefully by entering passcode, it will get restricted in just 1 min and will ask for passcode again and will block it again. (In ios 27, you can go to Screen time settings > Apps and websites > Search shortcuts app > Move to new category > Give category name '1 min time limit' > Dont select the default time shown and click on continue > Now, set a new time limit of 1 min and turn off Shared Time option. Thats it!) 

     |_ But what if someone directly deletes the Shortcut App? To block this, you must do the following: In ios 27, go to screen time settings > Apps and Websites > On top description, below that you will see a greyed option "Restrictions", click on it, then set Deleting Apps to 'Blocked'. Now, the app cannot be deleted.

5. Enable the feature : Stolen Device Protection
|_ Make sure to Enable the Stolen Device Protection in Settings App.
This feature forces to keep Iphone Tracking enabled. And if someone attempts to turn it off, it asks for face ID and adds 1-hour security delay.
This situation can happen if someone knows your iphone passcode, they may attempt to turn off Stolen Device Protection or iphone tracking, or change apple ID password. This feature stops from critical configuration changes happening by asking Face ID and adding 1-hour security delay.
The iphone will require a face ID scan and add an 1 hour security delay.

|_ What if someone attempts to turn off Face ID first since they have iphone passcode? A Face ID cannot be removed while Stolen Device Protection is enabled. And to turn off Stolen Device Protection, a Face ID authentication will be required and the person will be unable to make unwanted changes.

|_ A very quick tip : You can COMPLETELY block the editing of Face ID and Passcode in settings app by blocking it in screen time settings ( In ios 27, go to screen time settings, scroll to bottom > Content & Privacy Restrictions > scoll to bottom > Allow changes to Settings > Set Face id & passcode to 'Dont Allow').

6. Setting a SMS Trigger to trigger iphone lock and play a high volume warning sound that this iphone has been stolen (also with whatsapp trigger but it can be done via notification only for whatsapp).

7. Note: If the iphone is rebooted, all the automations will be stopped and will be enabled only after iphone is unlocked once. But it will lock your iphone and thief cannot open iphone.
