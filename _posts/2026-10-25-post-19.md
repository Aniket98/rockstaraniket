## \### SECURITY PLAN - IPHONE \###

Before starting, understand the point that our main objective is to first safeguard the data and this can be done by "LOCKING" the iphone. Iphone is built to be highly secured and encrypted.
Once the iphone is locked, atleast your data is safe somehow. Then, next objective will be to track and get back our iphone.

But this is not as simple as it sounds.

If the thief has secretly seen the iphone password you entered earlier, he can Unlock your Iphone (if you don't use face ID as default to unlock iphone, so you might have habit to type passwords in public).
Do Not Assume that thief has not seen your password secretly. Today's thief are smart. You even don't know how many people are watching you scroll reels from behind in public places, how will you make sure someone has not seen your password? I see everyday people watching reels or movies in stranger's phones, I also do 🤣.

Or worst case, if you are standing near Metro Train door, and thief snatches & runs away while iphone was Unlocked.

Also, iphone is tracked via officially website [https://icloud.com/find](https://icloud.com/find) and can be put into lock mode /or erase the iphone. The lock mode is a special mode that locks your iphone \+ disables many iphone services/features leaving iphone in a minimal operable state.

But this has a LIMITATION.
If the iphone is not connected to internet (maybe thief turns on flight mode or removes sim card), then iphone can only be tracked.
But cannot be Locked or Erased via [https://icloud.com/find](https://icloud.com/find).

This means if iphone is in UNLOCKED state, it will remain in unlocked state.
And the thief can go through your iphone apps and data.

To overcome this limitation, we need to build various custom automations inside iphone to LOCK it even without internet.

So, you must be prepared in all cases how to safeguard yourself even in worst cases, which we will discuss in this security plan.

The main flow should be : 

<span style="color:orange; font-size: 1.3em;"> **ASAP LOCK the Iphone by custom self-built automations**</span>

The last part to ERASE is important according to me (although its optional to you if you believe you can get back your iphone in time).
If the iphone gets switched off or become inaccessible to internet, then you cannot lock iphone via icloud.com/find, although you can still track it.

Also, in lock mode set via icloud.com/find, once the iphone is unlocked by someone (if thief knows your password), the lock mode will release. And your iphone will become vulnerable again and you need to set the lock mode again. That's why i said ERASING iphone remotely should be crucial.

So, you need many ways (that i have written below) to LOCK the Iphone to make the thief "PARALYZED" as much as possible.

Also, in end, I implemented a SMS Trigger to lock the iphone remotely and play a high volume sound that will speak/scream that "Iphone is stolen" multiple times. 

#### 1.  Have the habit to use FACE Id to unlock iphone.

Many people use 4 digit code to unlock iphone.
This is the worst kind of security. Such passcodes are very easy to get identified by thief in public places. Even with your finger movement someone can guess your passcode.

Always enable alpha-numeric long password.
And then enable FACE Id for daily use. And as a fallback, the alpha-numneric password can be used. (Make sure to type this password if needed very secretly and not openly & carelessly).

This step is extremely important, else it will be harder to protect your data if the iphone passcode gets leaked, that we will see more in next sections. Because otherwise even if manage to lock iphone, it can still be easily unlocked again by the thief.

#### 2. Locking and Tracking Lost iphone:

Iphone can be tracked on [https://icloud.com/find](https://icloud.com/find) and you can put iphone in lock mode with a message display to contact a number you set. This will instantly lock your iphone & disable many iphone services and a message will print on the lock screen that you set via website shown above.

\|_ Make sure you have apple ID and password saved already with you safely (if not done already).
Otherwise you will be unable to do anything.

\|_ This "putting iphone in lock mode via icloud" only works if your iphone is able to connect to internet. Hence, it is very important to not let thief turn off your internet, which we will see how to achieve in next sections. And we will also see how to lock iphone even if internet is somehow made inaccessible.

#### 3. Another situation : What if thief turns off iphone completely?

The iphone can still be tracked even when iphone is switched off.
And since iphone has been switched off, on next startup it will ask password. So, your data is safe in locked state of Iphone (as long as the password is not known to thief as discussed in POINT 1).

But with internet off, you will not be able to put iphone in lock mode (via icloud.com/find) and ERASE iphone.

What will happen if you go to [https://icloud.com/find](https://icloud.com/find) and enable lock mode? It will go in Pending state. Then, as soon as iphone is turned on and connected to internet, it will then go in lock mode and you can erase it as well.

#### 4. Thief never enables internet

But what if thief never enables internet? He may attempt so by putting phone in flight mode or turning off mobile data or wifi from lock screen. To avoid such thing happening, make sure to set your iphone setting as below:

\|_ Go to iphone settings and Dont allow to open Control Center from Lock Screen.

Settings > Face ID & Passcode > Enter Passcode > Allow Access When Locked > Set Control Center **OFF**

#### 5. And what if your iphone was in UNLOCKED State when the iphone was stolen? The thief can then turn off mobile data or put in airplane mode.

If iphone is stolen in unlocked state, then the very first thing the thief might do is turn off mobile data or wifi or put in flight mode, either via Control Center or Settings app. To avoid this thing to happen, you must already set in-built iphone automations (using Shortcuts app) to turn ON the mobile data or remove flight mode automatically the very moment someone changes tries to disable them and just after that iphone should get automatically locked. These automations are given below.

\|_ Before you start with automations, just a point...

You might have already enabled face lock on important apps.
But not everyone enables it. Maybe you have not enabled it to feel more smoothness/comfort in daily life. It's a trade off between comfort and security layer. I leave that decision to you whether to keep it enabled or disabled.

But other than this, as discussed, there are few things a thief might do in unlocked state, for which we need to setup below automations to add safety:-

\|____ Build and Enable these automations via in-built Shortcuts app:

      i) Lock the iphone automatically when Settings app is opened.

      The thief might attempt to turn mobile/cellular data off or put in flight mode by opening Settings app.

      Open shortcuts app, and set below automation flow:

      When Settings is opened > Turn Airplane mode off > Turn Cellular Data on > Lock the screen.

      But what if you wish to open Settings app yourself? Then the iphone will get locked for you as well. So what to do? The answer is simply unlock screen and when you unlock screen you will be inside Settings app and you can do your changes. But for strangers they will not be able to unlock screen and will get stuck.

     ii) Since settings app will be blocked instantly, stranger cannot turn off mobile data or enable airplane mode. But someone can still do so from the Control Center from if your iphone was stolen in unlocked state.

     To block this, do following:
          |_ a) BLOCKING Cellular/Mobile Data: --> In ios 27, go to screen time settings, scroll to bottom > Content & Privacy Restrictions > scoll to bottom > Allow changes to Settings > Set cellular data to 'Dont Allow'. Now, from the control center the cellular data cannot be turned off. If you yourself need to switch cellular data on/off, you can do so from settings app since you can pass through via screen lock.

          |_ b) BLOCKING Airplane Mode: --> In ios 27, open Shortcuts app, and set up below automation:

           When Airplane Mode is turned on > Turn Airplane mode off > Turn Cellular Data on > Lock the screen.

     iii) Very Important : Make sure the Shortcuts cannot be opened by someone. You cannot enable normal face id protection on this app as it is a system app. So, you must do following, in Settings App, go to Screen Time settings, add a screen time passcode. Then, from the screen time app list, block the Shortcuts app. Now, the shortcut apps cannot be opened and will require a passcode to unlock. 

     |_ Critical Note: If you ever try to open this blocked Shortcuts app, you will need to enter the passcode and open the app.
     But now the app will become unblocked forever. Either you must block it manually again, or a better way is to set a Time Allow of 0-minute to this shortcuts app in the Screen Time settings, this way when you open app forcefully by entering passcode it will ask to set 15min approval and you can do automation works, then after 15min it will get restricted & blocked again and will ask for passcode.
     (In ios 27, you can go to Screen time settings > Apps and websites > Search shortcuts app > Move to new category > Give category name '0 min time limit' > Dont select the default time shown and click on continue > Now, set a new time limit of 0 min and turn off Shared Time option. Thats it!) 

     |_ But what if someone directly deletes the Shortcut App? To block this, you must do the following: In ios 27, go to screen time settings > Apps and Websites > Below the topmost description, you will see a greyed option "Restrictions", click on it, then set Deleting Apps to 'Blocked'. Now, the app cannot be deleted.

#### 6. Enable the feature : Stolen Device Protection

\|_ Make sure to Enable the Stolen Device Protection in Settings App.

This feature forces to keep Iphone Tracking in enabled state. And if someone attempts to turn it off, it asks for face ID and adds 1-hour security delay.
This situation can happen if someone knows your iphone passcode, they may attempt to turn off Stolen Device Protection or iphone tracking, or change apple ID password. This feature stops from critical configuration changes happening by asking Face ID and adding 1-hour security delay.

\|_ What if someone attempts to turn off Face ID first since they have iphone passcode? A Face ID cannot be removed while Stolen Device Protection is enabled. And to turn off Stolen Device Protection, a Face ID authentication will be required and the person will be unable to make unwanted changes.

\|_ A very quick tip : You can COMPLETELY block the editing of Face ID and Passcode in settings app by blocking it in screen time settings ( In ios 27, go to screen time settings, scroll to bottom > Content & Privacy Restrictions > scoll to bottom > Allow changes to Settings > Set Face id & passcode to 'Dont Allow').

#### 7. Setting a SMS Trigger to trigger iphone lock

We can set a Shortcuts app automation to get triggered on a SMS and lock the iphone automatically. Additionally, play a high volume warning sound that this iphone has been stolen mutiple times.

Suppose you are in metro/train/bus/etc, and someone snatches away your iphone in UNLOCKED state.
You can quickly ask the nearby person to send a quick SMS to your mobile number. And the automation will trigger and the iphone will get locked.

The iphone will then start screming "Iphone is stolen" multiple times.
This can panic the thief and he can throw away the iphone in public. The automation also takes care if thief tries to lower down volume by volume buttons. The automation automatically sets the volume again to 100% and starts screaming again.
